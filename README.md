## What you can do

- **Analyse any round.** Record live with your phone's camera, or pick a video
  from your gallery (up to 8 minutes). Choose the sport and the rules (IBJJF,
  IBJJF with reversals, submission only, folkstyle, freestyle or MMA) and get
  a full scorecard for Fighter 1 and Fighter 2. Videos are compressed on your
  phone before upload, at the same frame rate and picture quality.
- **See what happened.** A timeline you can tap to jump through the video,
  highlight clips, head-to-head stats, a breakdown of every technique and
  coaching notes.
- **Climb the ranked ladder.** Challenge other fighters, record the match,
  and once you both confirm the result your rank moves: from Bronze /
  White Belt up to Grandmaster / Black Belt. Earn titles like Submission
  Wizard, Takedown Machine and Iron Chin.
- **Train with friends.** Add friends, share your scorecards and highlights,
  and follow their progress in your feed.
- **Stay in control.** Accounts are private until you choose otherwise. Your
  data and videos are never sold or shared with data brokers, and you can
  download or delete everything from Settings.
- **Use any AI.** Analyse with MatIQ's AI, or bring your own key for
  Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Together or any
  OpenAI-compatible service and pick the model.
- **A clean community.** Posts, names and bios go through a word filter,
  the AI checks recordings and profile pictures for 18+ content, and people
  who break the rules are banned, along with networks only they use.

## Install it on your phone

You need an Android phone with **Android 7.0 or newer** and about 250 MB of
free space.

1. **Download.** On your phone, open the
   [download link](https://github.com/JawharSas/MatiQ/releases/download/v1.7.0-pre-beta/MatIQ-1.7.0-android.apk).
   Your browser saves `MatIQ-1.7.0-android.apk`. If it warns that the file
   might be harmful, tap **Download anyway**.
2. **Open the file.** Tap the download notification, or open the **Files** /
   **Downloads** app and tap `MatIQ-1.7.0-android.apk`.
3. **Allow the install.** The first time, Android asks to allow installs from
   your browser or Files app: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this
   source**, then go back.
4. **Install.** Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect says the app is from
   an unknown developer, tap **More details → Install anyway**. You see this
   because MatIQ isn't on the Play Store yet.
5. **Open MatIQ** from your home screen or app drawer.

## Get started

1. **Create your account.** The quickest way: tap **Continue with Google**,
   pick your Google account, enter your birth month and year, tick the box
   to agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy and tap **Continue**. You're in;
   change your username any time under **Profile → Edit profile**.

   Or with your email: tap **Create an account**, then enter your email,
   a password (at least 12 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase
   letter, a number and a symbol), a username, the name people will see and
   your birth month and year. Tick the box to agree to the Terms and Privacy
   Policy and tap **Create account**. You need to be at least 13.
2. **Confirm your email** (email sign-up only). Open the link MatIQ emails you **on the same
   phone**, then sign in.
3. **Analyse your first round.** Open the **Analyze** tab, pick the sport,
   the rules and who's Fighter 1 and Fighter 2, then **Record live** or
   **Upload video**. Results appear in the **Matches** tab, usually within a
   few minutes.
4. **Go ranked.** In the **Ranked** tab, tap **Find an opponent** or
   challenge a friend. After the match, both of you confirm the result.
5. **Protect your account.** In **Profile → Settings → Two-factor
   authentication**, link an authenticator app so sign-ins need a code.

**For the best results:** keep the phone steady and both fighters fully in
frame, in good light. Scores are a training aid, not an official decision.

## Questions

**Is there an iPhone version?**
Not yet. MatIQ is Android-only during the pre-beta.

**Why does my phone warn me about the app?**
Android shows that warning for every app installed from outside the Play
Store. Only download MatIQ from the links on this page.

**How do I update?**
Download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one.
Your account, matches and rank are saved online, so nothing is lost.

**How do I delete my account or get a copy of my data?**
In the app, go to **Profile → Settings**. **Export my data** creates a file you
can save or share. To delete everything, type `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` under
**Delete account** and tap **Delete my account**; your profile, matches and
videos are removed.

**Can I make the text bigger or turn on high contrast?**
Yes: **Settings → Accessibility** (also on the sign-in screen) has text size,
high contrast and reduced motion. MatIQ also follows your phone's font size,
TalkBack / VoiceOver and Remove animations. Tell us at the support email in
that screen if anything is hard to use.

**Why do you ask for my birth month and year?**
MatIQ is for people aged 13 and over, and the law (COPPA) requires us to
check. It's never shown on your profile.

**Why was my video refused?**
MatIQ checks every video on your phone before uploading it and only accepts
real MP4 or MOV videos. A file that isn't a video, is damaged, or has extra
data hidden inside is refused, and the message says which. Videos recorded
with your phone's camera always work; so does recording live in MatIQ.

**Why wasn't my post shared?**
It includes words MatIQ doesn't allow (sexual content, slurs, harassment or
threats). Change the wording and post again. Three posts like that in 30 days
ban the account; talking about chokes, mounts and armbars is fine.

**Why is my account banned?**
Sexual content or nudity in a video or profile picture bans an account
straight away, and so do repeated posts that break the rules. If you think
it's a mistake, tap **Contact support** on the ban screen. Shared networks,
like a gym's Wi-Fi, are never blocked because of someone else's ban.

**Can I use my own AI, like ChatGPT or Gemini?**
Yes: **Settings → AI provider**. Paste a key from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini,
Grok, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Together or any OpenAI-compatible service.
MatIQ recognises the key, lists the models it can use and tests the one you
pick before saving it. Pick a model that can read images. Analyses with your
key are billed to your account with that provider and don't use your monthly
MatIQ analyses.

**Someone posted my video or footage of me.**
Open the post, tap **Report** and choose **Copyright infringement** or
**Something else**. Copyright notices go to our designated agent; see the
Copyright Policy in **Settings → Copyright and DMCA**.

**Something isn't working.**
This is a pre-beta, so expect a few rough edges. Make sure you have the
latest version from this page, then try again.
