## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-2.6.0-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and sign in with Google or your email, or create an
   account.

## What's new

- **Weekly goals.** On your profile, tap **Set goals** and choose targets
  for the week: rounds, wins, takedowns, submission attempts, guard passes
  and sweeps. Your rounds fill them in, and a streak counts the weeks you
  hit every goal.
- **Activity.** The bell at the top of the feed shows friend requests,
  ranked challenges, results to confirm, likes and comments, with a red dot
  when something is new. Tap one to open it.
- **Notifications.** **Settings → Notifications** has a switch for each kind.
  Pushes to your phone are on the way: until they arrive, everything shows
  in Activity.
- **Gyms.** Join your gym with its code, or start one and share the code.
  Your gym has its own feed, a leaderboard for each sport and a member list.
  Find it on your profile.
- **Progress charts.** The Matches tab shows your average points, time in
  control and win rate by month, and how this month compares.

## Good to know

- Joining a gym shows your name and ranked ratings to its members. You can
  leave at any time.
- Scores come from AI: treat them as a training aid, not an official
  decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
