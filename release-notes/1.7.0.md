## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.7.0-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and tap **Continue with Google**, or **Create an account**
   to use your email.

## What's new

- **Videos up to 8 minutes**: record or upload rounds of up to 8 minutes.
- **Much smaller uploads**: before a video is uploaded, your phone compresses
  it once with HEVC (H.265), the format phone cameras use for "high
  efficiency" video. A typical Android recording goes up about three times
  smaller, so uploads finish sooner and use less mobile data.
- **Same picture, same frame rate**: 60 FPS stays 60 FPS, and the picture
  keeps its size up to 1080p, with a bit rate chosen for its size and frame
  rate so fast scrambles stay sharp. Videos that are already compact, like
  an iPhone's own, are uploaded as they are.
- **Clearer progress**: the screen shows whether MatIQ is checking,
  compressing or uploading your video.
- **Light, Dark and Midnight**: pick a theme in **Settings → Appearance**,
  or let MatIQ follow your phone. Midnight is true black, easy on the eyes
  at night and on OLED screens.
- **A Settings tab**: everything in one place, including your email,
  password, Google, two-factor, theme, accessibility, AI provider, your data
  and help.
- **Add Google to your account**: signed up with email? Connect Google in
  **Settings → Google** and sign in either way. Signed up with Google? Set a
  password in **Settings → Password**.
- **Change or reset your password**: change it in Settings, or tap
  **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen to get a code by email.
- **New home for your data**: MatIQ moved to new servers in Frankfurt
  (Neon), with sign-in by Clerk. Creating an account now sends a code to
  your email instead of a link, and a new phone is confirmed with a code
  by email.

## Good to know

- **Using your own AI key?** Add it again in **Settings → AI provider**:
  saved keys couldn't come along to the new servers (they stay encrypted
  and are deleted with the old ones).
- **Two-factor on?** After updating, check **Settings → Two-factor
  authentication**. If it says it's off, turn it on again with your
  authenticator app: it takes a minute, and you get new backup codes.

- Compressing takes a little while on long videos (it runs while frames are
  sampled for the analysis, which uses the original video).
- Videos larger than 1080p (4K) are stored at 1080p, the size MatIQ plays
  and analyses.
- No compression can be truly lossless at a third of the size; this one is
  set so the difference isn't visible in normal playback.
- This is a pre-beta: expect a few rough edges, and treat scores as a training
  aid, not an official decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
