## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.8.6-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and sign in, or tap **Sign up** to create an account with
   your email or Google.

## What's new

- **Black and red**: MatIQ now opens in black and red, with red for the
  things you tap, trophies and wins, and a new red and black app icon.
  Prefer it lighter? **Settings → Appearance** has a white theme, true
  black (Midnight) and your phone's setting. Every colour is checked for
  contrast in every theme.
- **Comments on posts**: tap the speech bubble under a post to read and
  write comments. Sharing a round? Switch off **Allow comments** before you
  post, and turn them on or off any time from your post's comments. You
  can delete any comment on your own posts.
- **A new home for your data**: MatIQ moved to new servers in Frankfurt
  (Neon), with sign-in by Clerk: email and password, Google, codes by
  email, two-factor and password resets, all on one screen.
- **Videos up to 8 minutes**: record or upload rounds of up to 8 minutes.
- **Much smaller uploads**: your phone compresses a video once with HEVC
  (H.265) before it's uploaded, usually to about a third of the size, with
  the same frame rate and picture size up to 1080p. The screen shows
  whether MatIQ is checking, compressing or uploading.
- **A Settings tab**: your account, theme (Light, Dark, Midnight or your
  phone's setting), accessibility, AI provider, your data and help, in one
  place.
- **Account and security**: **Settings → Account and security** manages your
  email addresses, password, Google, two-factor and the devices you're
  signed in on.
- **Faster to open**: screens you've seen before show straight away from
  this phone while the latest loads, content placeholders replace spinners,
  and likes change the moment you tap.
- **Steadier taps and typing**: a button can't send something twice, every
  button and row responds to your touch, and the keyboard never covers the
  field you're typing in.
- **A better first run**: an empty feed or friends list shows you where to
  start: analyse a round or find your training partners.

## Good to know

- **On MatIQ 1.6 or older?** Install this one: older versions can no longer
  save anything since the move. From now on, MatIQ tells you itself when an
  update is needed.
- **Using your own AI key?** Add it again in **Settings → AI provider**:
  saved keys couldn't come along to the new servers (they stay encrypted
  and are deleted with the old ones).
- **Two-factor on?** After updating, check **Settings → Account and
  security**. If it's off, turn it on again with your authenticator app; you
  get new backup codes. Your password and Google sign-in live there too.
- Compressing takes a little while on long videos, and videos larger than
  1080p (4K) are stored at 1080p, the size MatIQ plays and analyses.
- This is an alpha: expect a few rough edges, and treat scores as a training
  aid, not an official decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
