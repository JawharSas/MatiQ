## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.6.0-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and tap **Continue with Google**, or **Create an account**
   to use your email.

## What's new

- **Use any AI**: Settings → **AI provider**. Paste a key from Claude,
  ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Together or any
  OpenAI-compatible service. MatIQ recognises the key, shows the models it
  can use (the ones that can read images first), and tests the one you
  pick with one small picture before saving it. You can keep keys for
  several AIs and choose which one your analyses use.
- **The analysis setup shows your AI**: "Use my own AI: Gemini" with the
  model, so you know what will score the round.
- **Fairer bans**: when someone is banned, MatIQ only blocks networks that
  person alone uses. A gym's Wi-Fi or any network other people on MatIQ use
  is never blocked because of someone else.
- **A fresh look**:
  - Feed posts show the score as a scoreboard, one panel per fighter, with
    the result on top.
  - Matches shows your record (wins, losses, draws), groups rounds by month,
    and puts each analysed match's score and result on the card.
  - Profile has a new header with your matches, record and friends.

## Good to know

- Pick a model that can read images: MatIQ sends it frames from your
  recording. Analyses with your own key are billed to your account with that
  provider and don't use your monthly MatIQ analyses.
- Keys are encrypted on MatIQ's servers and never shown again or stored on
  your phone.
- This is a pre-beta: expect a few rough edges, and treat scores as a training
  aid, not an official decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
