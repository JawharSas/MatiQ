MatIQ's first full release: a new look from top to bottom, and a short tutorial to show you around.

## Download

**[MatIQ-2.5.8-android.apk](https://github.com/JawharSas/MatiQ/releases/download/v2.5.8/MatIQ-2.5.8-android.apk)**: Android 7.0 or newer. iPhone is coming later.

Already have MatIQ? Install this over it: your account, matches and rank
are kept.

## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-2.5.8-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and sign in with Google or your email, or create an
   account.

## What's new

- **A tutorial.** The first time you open this version, five short steps
  show you how to record a round, read your fight card, climb the ranks and
  share. You can skip it, and see it again any time from **Settings →
  Tutorial**.
- **A new look.** Black with red by default, plus Midnight (pure black) and
  White (snow white), with bold, sporty numbers and headings. Pick one in
  **Settings → Theme**.
- **A simpler bottom bar.** Five icons: Feed, Matches, **+** to record,
  Ranked (with a red dot when a challenge or result needs you) and your
  picture for Profile. Settings is behind the gear on your profile, and
  Friends is the people icon at the top of the feed.
- **Fight cards in the feed.** Every shared round shows the score in big
  numbers, a red bar under the winner and how it ended. Tap **Breakdown**
  for the full match. Report a post from its **...** button.
- **A new match screen.** The result at the top, then four tabs:
  **Overview** (the stats where someone had the edge, the key moments you
  can tap to replay, and the coach's notes), **Moments**, **Stats** and
  **Log**.
- **A new profile.** Your picture with your matches, record and friends,
  then your bio and your ranked card. **Edit profile** has your picture,
  names, bio and the Public account switch in one place.
- **Belt badges.** Ranks show the belt they stand for, and a black belt has
  its red bar.
- **Vibration.** A light tap when you switch tabs, like a post or start and
  stop a recording. Turn it off in **Settings → Accessibility → Vibration**.

## Good to know

- **Signed up with Google?** Sign in with **Continue with Google** or with
  a code sent to your email; your account has no password unless you set
  one in **Settings → Account and security**.
- Scores come from AI: treat them as a training aid, not an official
  decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
