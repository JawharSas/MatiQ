## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.3.1-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and tap **Continue with Google**, or **Create an account**
   to use your email.

## What's new

- **Continue with Google** on the sign-in and sign-up screens: pick your
  Google account and you're in, with no password and no confirmation email.
  New accounts start with your Google name; change your username any time in
  your profile.
- **Already signed up with email?** Continue with Google using the same
  address and you land in the same account.
- Sign-in links from emails now open the app straight away.

## Good to know

- MatIQ only receives your name and email address from Google, never your
  Google password.
- This is a pre-beta: expect a few rough edges, and treat scores as a training
  aid, not an official decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
