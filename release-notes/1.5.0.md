## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.5.0-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and tap **Continue with Google**, or **Create an account**
   to use your email.

## What's new

- **Community rules**: no sexual content or nudity, no hate, slurs,
  harassment or threats. They're in the Terms (Settings → Terms of Service).
- **Word filter**: names, bios, match titles, dispute notes and posts are
  checked for words MatIQ doesn't allow, even when letters are spaced out,
  swapped for numbers or stretched. A post that breaks the rules isn't
  shared and the app tells you why. Three of those in 30 days ban the
  account. Training talk like "rear naked choke" is fine.
- **18+ check**: the AI that scores your rounds also checks the frames for
  sexual content. If it finds any, the recording is removed and the account
  is banned straight away.
- **Profile pictures are checked first**: a new picture only shows up for
  other people once it has passed the same check.
- **Bans that stick**: a banned account is signed out, everything it posted
  is hidden, and the networks it used are blocked for 30 days, so a new
  account on the same connection doesn't get around it. MatIQ only keeps
  a scrambled fingerprint of those network addresses, never the addresses.
- **If you're banned**, MatIQ shows you why and how to ask for a review.
