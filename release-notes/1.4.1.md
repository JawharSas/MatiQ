## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.4.1-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and tap **Continue with Google**, or **Create an account**
   to use your email.

## What's new

- **Accessibility settings** (Settings → Accessibility, also on the sign-in
  screen): bigger text in four sizes, high contrast, and reduced motion.
  MatIQ also follows your phone's font size, TalkBack and Remove animations.
- **Easier to see and use**: stronger contrast, visible outlines on fields,
  switches and options, a focus outline for keyboards, and every button
  labelled for screen readers. Checked against WCAG 2.2 AA on every screen.
- **Turn your phone sideways**: MatIQ now works in landscape too.
- **Sign-up asks for your birth month and year** (MatIQ is for ages 13 and
  up) and uses one box to agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy, unticked
  until you tick it. Sign-up, the policy screen, Share, Report and disputes
  have a **Cancel** button.
- **Report a post**: tap **Report** on any post for copyright problems or
  anything else. The Copyright Policy is in Settings → Copyright and DMCA.
- **Videos are checked before they upload.** When you pick a video, MatIQ
  reads the whole file on your phone (you'll see "Checking the video…") and
  only uploads real MP4 or MOV videos. Other files, damaged videos and files
  with anything extra hidden inside are refused with a message saying why.
  Recordings made in the app are checked the same way.
- **Uploaded videos are checked again** by MatIQ's server before anyone you
  share a match with can watch them.
- **Sign-in protection**: after 5 wrong passwords in a row, the app makes you
  wait before trying again (30 seconds, longer if it keeps happening).
- Behind the scenes: stricter security headers, automatic checks for leaked
  keys and vulnerable libraries, and a security fix in a validation library
  the server uses.
