Sign-in fixed: your account opens again after you sign in.

## Download

**[MatIQ-2.1.4-android.apk](https://github.com/JawharSas/MatiQ/releases/download/v2.1.4-alpha/MatIQ-2.1.4-android.apk)**: Android 7.0 or newer. iPhone is coming later.

Already have MatIQ? Install this over it: your account, matches and rank
are kept.

## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-2.1.4-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ** and sign in with Google or your email, or create an
   account.

## What's new

- **Sign-in works again**: after signing in, MatIQ 1.8.6 could stop at
  "You don't have permission to do that." This was fixed on our servers, so
  it works on 1.8.6 too, and every release now signs in to the live servers
  before it's published.
- **Try again**: if your account ever doesn't open after you sign in, the
  screen says so, shows an error code you can send us, and has a **Try
  again** button. MatIQ also tries again by itself when you come back to the
  app.

## Good to know

- **Still on the error screen in 1.8.6?** Tap **Sign out**, then sign in
  again. Or install this version.
- **Signed up with Google?** Sign in with **Continue with Google** or with
  a code sent to your email; your account has no password unless you set
  one in **Settings → Account and security**.
- This is an alpha: expect a few rough edges, and treat scores as a training
  aid, not an official decision.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
