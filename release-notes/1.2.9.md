## Install

1. Open the download link **on your Android phone**. If the browser warns
   about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Tap the finished download (or find `MatIQ-1.2.9-android.apk` in your
   **Files** / **Downloads** app).
3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source: **Settings → Allow from
   this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **More
   details → Install anyway**; you see this because MatIQ isn't on the Play
   Store yet.
5. Open **MatIQ**, tap **Create account** and confirm your email from the same
   phone.

## What's in this version

- **Analyse any round**: record live or upload a video, choose the sport and
  rules (IBJJF, IBJJF with reversals, submission only, folkstyle, freestyle,
  MMA) and get a scorecard for Fighter 1 and Fighter 2.
- **Live scoring**: a running score appears while you record.
- **Match breakdown**: tap-to-jump timeline, highlight clips, head-to-head
  stats, technique breakdown and coaching notes.
- **Ranked ladder**: challenge other fighters, confirm results together and
  climb from Bronze / White Belt to Grandmaster / Black Belt. Earn titles like
  Submission Wizard, Takedown Machine and Iron Chin.
- **Friends and feed**: share scorecards and highlights with friends or
  everyone. Accounts are private until you choose otherwise.
- **Two-factor sign-in**: protect your account with an authenticator app.
- **Your data stays yours**: never sold or shared with data brokers. Export
  or delete everything from Settings.

## Good to know

- This is a pre-beta: expect a few rough edges, and treat scores as a training
  aid, not an official decision.
- For the best results, keep the phone steady with both fighters in frame and
  good light.
- To update later, install the new version over this one. Your account,
  matches and rank are saved online.
- Only download MatIQ from this page.
